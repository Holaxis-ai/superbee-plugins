# Place comparison extension example

This recipe owns only Place Comparison. The base owns Research Claim and Research
Observation. Similar addresses, map positions and household names are proposed
connections rather than evidence of identity. Keep the assessment on a common
Claim and the literal material on Observations. Other domains can copy this small
pattern with their own fields and relationships instead of inheriting a base Kind.
