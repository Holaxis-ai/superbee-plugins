# My Home

Installs three disjoint Kinds and one operating reference. Start with one useful Home or Asset; no property records, addresses, model, tasks, credentials or execution are installed. Optional maintenance and costs recipes link into these Kinds rather than redefining them.

Use the existing recipe-set structural preflight for a selected composition. Manifest dependency resolution is not assumed. Examples and executable qualification remain outside the recipe payload.
