# Civic evidence to resident update handoff

Keep immutable official records separate from Civic Findings. Findings describe resident impact and required checks; they are not published updates. Start with worthwhile project, service, policy and participation developments, not a recap of every voucher or roll call.

For a publisher with a Civic Update kind, map only after checking its current schema. Example mappings: actual project authorization -> historical approved stage; preliminary discussion -> under-review or informational, never adopted; conditional event approval -> research lead pending final approval and current event details. A funding resolution does not prove construction, expenditure or completion. A budget's total appropriation is not automatically the municipality's permanent net cost.

Before handoff, check subsequent minutes/official project pages for superseding decisions, the original record approval status, exact conditions/effective dates, reader relevance, duplicate existing coverage and temporal suitability. Preserve an auditable page/heading citation. A stale event becomes historical or rejected rather than a current Event. Never invent public participation instructions or a deadline.

A Here & Main pilot may draft field mappings and plain-language copy here, but changing its live bundle still follows its separate supported content contribution path, genuine independent source/editor/visual review and normal publisher/release gates. Do not create a second publishing pipeline, trigger the stopped curator, publish research leads or broaden workload authority.
